# Sprint 5 — Hub Gene Functional Interpretation

## Objective

Interpret the biological significance of the hub genes identified from the Cytoscape protein–protein interaction network.

---

## Input

- ppi/hub_gene_analysis.csv
- Cytoscape network analysis results

---

## Methods

- Ranked hub genes based on Degree centrality.
- Reviewed biological functions of the highest-ranked genes.
- Related hub genes to known inflammatory and epithelial signaling pathways.
- Summarized biological significance in the context of the RNA-seq experiment.

## Biological Interpretation

The hub genes indicate strong activation of inflammatory signaling together with epithelial remodeling. IL1B, PTGS2 and CXCR4 are major regulators of immune and inflammatory responses, whereas KRT5 and KRT14 are structural epithelial markers. PPARG links metabolic regulation with inflammatory processes. Together, these findings suggest coordinated regulation of immune signaling and epithelial tissue remodeling in the experimental condition.
